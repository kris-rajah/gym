The Cut.

Hypertrophy-first, strength-preserving. 90 kg → 81 kg. Undulating 4-week waves across Push / Pull / Legs plus Sunday conditioning.

Principles
01 · Tiered Session

Power → Large → Medium → Light

Every session opens with a CNS primer, moves through heavy barbell compounds, transitions to machine/DB compounds, and finishes with high-rep isolations at 12–15 reps.

02 · Wave Periodisation

Load 1 → 2 → 3 → 4

Each 4-week block rolls through deload (12–15), moderate (9–11), heavy (6–8), max (2–5). Light tier always stays at 12–15 regardless of the week's load.

03 · Context-Aware

Office 45 min · Home 60 min

Tuesdays run the office-gym constraint (no cardio, tight session). Wed/Fri/Sat are full home sessions. Sundays are conditioning + 20 min steady-state cardio.
